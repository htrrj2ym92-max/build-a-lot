A playful neighbourhood building game

Lot by Lot

Buy empty lots, build houses, earn rent and grow eight streets into the best addresses in the county. Finish a street by its Expert day to earn the gold key.

  1. Buy a lot. Lots with a red sign are for sale. Click one, then buy it.
  2. Build. Houses need materials and crew time. Materials arrive in 15 seconds; a Sawmill halves their price and delivery time.
  3. Earn rent. Tenants pay daily, but damaged houses earn nothing. A Workshop lets you inspect and repair them.
  4. Grow. Hire crew, add stars, paint or landscape houses, sell properties, and build Sawmills and Workshops.

Keys: Space pause, 1–3 game speed, Esc deselect, M mute sound.
